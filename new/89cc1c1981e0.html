The 2026 Ford Explorer Tremor 4WD in Star White Metallic Tri-Coat is an exceptionally rugged and capable three-row SUV designed to handle off-the-beaten-path adventures without compromising on everyday refinement.

Performance is driven by a potent 3.0L EcoBoost V6 engine paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission and intelligent 4WD. Equipped with front tow hooks, auxiliary light switches and wiring, an engine block heater, and a Class III trailer tow package, this Explorer Tremor is built to tackle demanding terrain while delivering a combined fuel consumption rating of 12.4 L/100 km.

Inside, the cabin features Onyx Miko suede seating with multicontour heated and ventilated front seats, heated second-row seats, and power-folding third-row seats for ultimate flexibility. The high-tech cockpit centers around a 13.2-inch touchscreen and a 12.3-inch digital cluster, complemented by a 14-speaker B&O sound system, ambient lighting, and a panoramic fixed-glass roof with a power shade.

Utility and safety are expertly integrated with a power liftgate, 360-degree camera system, adaptive cruise control, lane keeping system, pre-collision assist with automatic emergency braking, and a perimeter alarm to ensure total confidence on every journey.
